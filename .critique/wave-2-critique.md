Wave 1 had a static blue gradient.

## Single biggest remaining gap
**The fountain's water needs to mound up into a dome above the rim, and the basin's inner walls need to be light stone gray, not slate.** Right now in `buildings.js:353-377`:
- rim colour `0x8a8a82` (light) is correct, but `basin` colour is `0x5a5a55` (very dark slate) and is taller than the rim (`basin.position.y = 0.22` with height 0.45 → top at 0.45, vs rim top at 0.21), so the dark interior fills the entire visible bowl and reads as the fountain's body.
- water is `CylinderGeometry(1.5, 1.5, 0.04, 24)` at `y = 0.42`, which sits **inside** the basin top (0.45), not above the rim (0.21). So you never see water above stone — you only see a tiny blue sliver inside a brown well.

Fix: change `basin` to `color: 0xb8b6ad, height: 0.18` (a low light-gray inner sleeve that sits flush with the rim's top), then raise `water` to `y = 0.28` (just above the rim) and **add a second smaller `SphereGeometry(0.6, 16, 12)` hemispherical water mound at `y = 0.32`** tinted `0xb8e4ff` with high emissive — this single change turns "candle on brownie" into "fountain." Also drop the basin radius so the rim overhangs the water slightly (rim radius 1.95, basin radius 1.7 instead of 1.55).

## Secondary gaps (max 5)
1. **Trees still read as tall narrow snowman stacks instead of wide low AC broccoli-clouds.** In `world.js:295-296`, `trunkH = max(2.2, height*0.85)` and `canopyScale = 1.3` give trees taller than they are wide; AC reference `ac-tree-cf.jpg` is the opposite. Reduce `trunkH` to 1.5, raise `canopyScale` to 1.7, and add 1–2 wider side-lobe spheres (`r = baseR*1.1` offset `±0.7` horizontally at mid-canopy height) so the silhouette spreads laterally.
2. **Fence stringers look broken at corners.** `_makeVillagerHome` `buildings.js:218-238` builds each segment of the circle independently — so the rails stop at each picket-cluster boundary and the next segment's rail starts again, leaving visible gaps. Either build one continuous ring with `sides=32` and only place pickets (no per-segment rails), or extend the rail `length` by `0.1` on each end so adjacent segments overlap at the post.
3. **Dawn sky is deep red, not peach/pink/orange.** `world.js:557` `dawnColor = 0xffa070` lerped 0.45 produces a red sky in `critic3-dawn-sky.png`; AC dawns are warm yellow-pink. Drop dawn lerp to `0.30` and split: dawn = `0xffc88a` (peach), dusk = `0xd88aaa` (rose) so neither period goes fully saturated red.
4. **Stars are sparse and barely visible.** `world.js:603` uses `count = 280` at `size: 0.7, sizeAttenuation: false`. The `critic3-stars.png` and `critic2-night-sky.png` show maybe 6–8 visible specks per frame. Bump `count` to 1200 with `size: 1.0` and add a few larger `size: 2.0` sparkle stars (10–15 of them). Also enable `vertexColors` with a faint per-star colour jitter so they don't all read as identical white pixels.
5. **Pillar/spray on the fountain is too thin and short.** `buildings.js:379-400`: pillar `0.22→0.32 radius, 1.1 tall`; urn `0.62→0.32 radius, 0.5 tall`; spray `0.18 radius` opaque sphere. AC fountain's central column is thicker and the water ball is larger. Increase pillar bottom radius to 0.45, urn bottom to 0.7, and spray sphere to 0.3 with `transparent: true, opacity: 0.65` so it reads as water mist not a marble.

## What's now genuinely close
- The **horizon** in any wide noon/dawn shot genuinely reads as AC; you'd have to be told there used to be a black band to notice it.
- The **roof gable** is structurally correct — at any distance you immediately read it as "AC pitched roof" rather than "awning."
- The **color palette and bench layout** around the plaza read as deliberate AC homage (warm beige walls, varied roof colours, painted awning, plaza benches, signpost). A casual observer would clock this as a small AC-style town.
- The **dawn/dusk colour shift** does convincingly turn the world "warm" — even if the exact hue is too saturated red, the *concept* of a warm twilight is now visible in every screenshot.
