Wave 6 — Villager Heart critic: PASS

Villagers now feel like people with routines instead of random wanderers:

  • Daily schedule — each of the 5 villagers follows a deterministic routine: wake → morning waypoint (front yard) → plaza → evening waypoint → sleep at home. Hour thresholds stored per species (Maple the bear is up earliest as a baker; Coral the octopus is the night owl).
  • Gift reaction — walk up to a villager with a gift in your active hotbar slot, prompt changes to "Press E to give [item]". Hearts spawn above their head, friendship goes up by gift.value × multiplier. Each villager has a favoriteGift (Maple→flower, Finn→bug, Pebble→fossil, Coral→shell, Hazel→acorn) — matching gives 2×.
  • Branching dialogue — greet() picks from a tiered pool based on friendship: <100 = stranger greetings; ≥100 = + anecdotes; ≥250 = + inside jokes and dinner invites. 45+ unique dialogue outcomes across the 5 villagers.
  • Birthdays — each villager has a birthday day-of-year; on their birthday a procedural � cake sprite (drawn on a 64×64 canvas, no emoji font) bobs above their head, and any gift given that day earns 3× friendship.
  • Bunny Day — Spring day 7 spawns a hidden procedural bunny NPC in the plaza (egg body, upright ears with pink inner-ear cylinders, fluffy tail). Gift it a flower to trigger the celebration cutscene + 500 bells + 3 shells + 5 flowers. One-time per save.
  • Save v2 — bumped storage key to willowbrook.save.v2 with v1 → v2 migration; now serializes per-villager friendship + first-meeting + Bunny Day flag so all Villager Heart progress survives reload.
